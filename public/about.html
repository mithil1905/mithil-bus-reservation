Architectural Foundations of the System

🚌

Fleet Management (buses)

Full CRUD administration of vehicle models (Volvo 9600, Scania, BharatBenz), seating capacities (2+1 sleeper berths), and route pricing calculated in Indian Rupees (₹).

👥

Yatri Records (passengers)

Full CRUD administration of passenger profiles, with verified Indian ID proofs (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID), demographics, and emergency contact details.

🎫

PNR Subsystem (bookings)

Full CRUD reservation engine that generates unique PNR identifiers, locks real-time sleeper seats, calculates fares, and issues printable electronic tickets.
